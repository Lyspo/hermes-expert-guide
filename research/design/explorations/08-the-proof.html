The loop that writes itself

Self-improvement is sold as a disposition. What ships is three mechanical parts: a counter that increments once per tool-calling iterationSRC, a threshold, and a forked agent that is forbidden from writing to your sessionSRC.

The documentation says the review runs roughly every ten agent turnsDOC — which is an approximation of an iteration count, and the difference is why the loop can fire twice inside one request and never at all across an afternoon of chatting.

When it fires, you see one line, printed after your answerCAP. How reliably it fires across installs is not something anyone has measuredGAP.

---
term: 'Claim'
short: 'A worker''s atomic lease on a ready task, reclaimed only if the worker''s process has actually died.'
hermesVersion: v0.19.0
---

Expiry alone releases nothing. A live worker in a long model call has its claim extended, which makes reclaim a crash recovery rather than a timeout.

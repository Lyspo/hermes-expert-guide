The gateway is one process with one authorization chain and identical mechanics per
adapter, so the second platform you connect is a lookup rather than a lesson. This is the
lookup.

## Delivery targets cron accepts

`origin` · `local` · `telegram` · `discord` · `slack` · `whatsapp` · `signal` · `matrix` ·
`email` · `sms` · `homeassistant` · `all`

A platform not on this list cannot receive scheduled output, whatever else its adapter
supports.

## What varies, and what does not

**Does not vary.** The authorization chain, the agent core, your toolsets, approval
configuration, per-chat session isolation, and slash-command dispatch. A capability
configured once applies everywhere — and so does a misconfiguration.

**Varies by platform.** Threading, voice, file size limits, reactions, inline keyboards,
streaming updates, and whether a group chat needs an explicit mention.

## Telegram, as the worked example

| Capability | Support |
|---|---|
| Voice in and out | Yes, needs `ffmpeg` |
| Images | Send and receive |
| Large files | A separate transport path; the ordinary bot API caps lower than expected |
| Threads | Forum topics, bindable to a skill |
| Reactions | Yes |
| Streaming | Edits one message bubble rather than posting many |
| Progress indicator | Pins your message at turn start, unpins on completion |
| Inline choices | `clarify` renders a keyboard, 600-second timeout |
| Group behaviour | Replies only when mentioned, by default |
| Webhook mode | Available, and **requires a secret** |

## Three that catch people

**Telegram privacy mode is on by default**, and after changing it you **must remove and
re-add the bot to every group**. The setting is cached per membership, so changing it does
nothing to groups the bot has already joined.

**Group chat ids are negative numbers.** A hand-written delivery target that lost the minus
sign silently goes nowhere.

**Connecting WhatsApp gives the agent your real phone number**, with no preview step.

## Notification volume

`important` restricts alerts to final responses and approval prompts, leaving tool progress
silent. On a phone that is almost always what you want, and it is not the default.

## Approvals, on a messaging surface

The gate becomes a message rather than a menu: the bot posts the command and you reply
`yes` or `no`. The 300-second timeout still applies and still fails closed, so a push
notification you do not see for six minutes is a denial.

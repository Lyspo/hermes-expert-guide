## Set these deliberately rather than by default

```yaml
approvals:
  mode: manual          # the default asks a model, not you
  timeout: 300          # fails closed
  cron_mode: deny
  deny:
    - "git push --force*"

agent:
  max_turns: 90
  disabled_toolsets: [browser, image_gen]

terminal:
  cwd: /srv/agent/project
```

`approvals.deny` is the only control here that is deterministic *and* yours. Keep a short
list per deployment.

## The three holes, stated

**The container backend removes the approval gate.** Four of six backends skip the
dangerous-command check, and your deny rules are enforced only on host-reaching backends.

**The dashboard's plugin routes bypass its authentication**, by design, on the assumption
of a localhost bind.

**The egress proxy does not cover every provider.** Bedrock SigV4 and Vertex service
accounts keep real credentials inside the sandbox, and the proxy does not stop exfiltration
to an allowlisted host.
